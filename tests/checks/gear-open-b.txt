# Searched from side B, Open as setup keeps side A and puts the set on side B. B wears a Normal Bow in place of
# Chk's Oak Bow, and a search from B, the weapon alone, finds a javelin; the panel, on side B, shows that answer as
# current and opens it onto B. Side B is chosen on Red Slime's form, which has no answer: the choice is on the form,
# and Green Slime's answer shows over its own. Green Slime is the one searched: compare-gear-tab needs Red Slime
# without an answer. The panel's pool is set to every item, as the search's, or the answer isn't the panel's.
cs2 compare SlimeForest/RedSlime SlimeForest/GreenSlime a=current b=current b.equip=Weapon:NormalBow trials=200
cs2 wait timeout=9
cs2 gear SlimeForest/GreenSlime pool=every kinds=none seed=1 trials=30 short wait=0 lock=Helmet,Cape,Amulet,Torso,Legs,RingSlot1,Gloves,Boots,Offhand,Ammo equip=Weapon:NormalBow
expect reply "running:"
wait 9000
cs2 gear show
expect reply "Weapon: Normal Bow → Meteorite Javelin"
cs2 select SlimeForest/RedSlime
cs2 open simulate
wait 1200
click Cs2DetailTabGear
wait 800
click GearSearchPool
wait 300
click GearSearchPoolItem_1
wait 500
click GearSearchFromB
wait 500
cs2 select SlimeForest/GreenSlime
wait 3000
expect see "Weapon · replaces Normal Bow"
click GearSearchOpen
wait 1000
cs2 ui
expect reply "side a base=current changes=0"
expect reply "side b base=current changes=1 req=check equip=Weapon:MeteoriteJavelin"
click Cs2DetailTabRates
wait 300
